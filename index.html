WORLD 1

What is a node?

A node is a place where the wavefunction is exactly zero: ψ = 0. The probability of finding the electron is |ψ|², so at a node that probability is zero too. The electron can be on either side of a node, but never right on it.

1D · the string
nodes on the string

A guitar string vibrates in standing waves. The points that never move are its nodes. Every extra node costs energy, so the pitch goes up.

The fixed ends don't count as nodes, just like r = ∞ in an atom.

2D · the drum
nodal circles
nodal diameters

On a drumhead the nodes are lines, and they come in two kinds: circles, which depend on the distance from the center, and diameters, which depend on the angle.

WORLD 2

The rules of the game

Two quantum numbers decide everything. n sets how many nodes there are in total; ℓ decides how many of them are angular. The rest are radial.

total nodesn − 1

Grows with the shell: 1s has 0 nodes, 7s has 6.

angular nodesℓ

Planes or cones. They depend only on the shape: s has 0, p 1, d 2, f 3.

radial nodesn − ℓ − 1

Spheres. They're what's left: the total minus the angular ones.

s ℓ = 0p ℓ = 1d ℓ = 2f ℓ = 3ℓ runs from 0 to n − 1

The nucleus doesn't count

In p, d and f orbitals, ψ = 0 at r = 0. But that's a point, not a surface, so it isn't a radial node.

Neither does infinity

Far from the atom ψ gets close to zero, but it never crosses to the other sign. There's no node out there.

dz² has no nodal planes

Its 2 angular nodes are cones, at 54.7° and 125.3° from the z axis.

Radial nodes hide inside

From the outside, 1s and 2s look like the same sphere. You only see the inner shell if you cut the orbital open.

Phase is not charge

+ and − are the sign of ψ, not electric charges. |ψ|² is always positive.

Same n, same total

3s, 3p and 3d have 2 nodes each. Only the split between spheres and planes or cones changes.

    WORLD 4

    Radial nodes: onion layers

    R(r) is a polynomial of degree n − ℓ − 1 times a decaying exponential. That polynomial crosses zero n − ℓ − 1 times, and each crossing is a nodal sphere. As n goes up, more layers appear, one inside the other.

    pick ℓ
    √r scale: the center is stretched so the inner nodes are visible. Tap one to open it in the Nodescope.
    WORLD 5

    Angular nodes: planes and cones

    Y(θ,φ) doesn't depend on distance, so its nodes pass through the nucleus and cut all the way through the orbital: they are planes or cones. There are exactly ℓ of them, whatever n is. These are the 16 shapes with no radial nodes (n = ℓ + 1). Tap one to open it in the Nodescope.
